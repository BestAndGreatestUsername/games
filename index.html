Game Shelf

Five browser games. Pick one and play. Works best with a keyboard and mouse.

Farmer Tank: Keep Them In!

You're a fragile robot in an endless living world. Catch wacky creatures and grow the biggest farm.

WASD to move, Shift to sprint, left click to shoot, right click to throw, E to gather. Online co-op with a 5-letter code.

Swapshot

One swap per round. Make it count.

A puzzle card game. Swap any two cards in the robots' programs, then run the round. Every round can be won.

Play ▸

Tower of Babble

You are a baby. You can't walk. You have a shotgun.

Push the muzzle into things to shove yourself around, and click to fire. Recoil launches you. Climb the zombie tower.

Play ▸

Skill Issue

The most annoying platformer ever made. On purpose.

Arrows or A/D to move. Space, W, Up, Z or K to jump. Tap jumps high, hold jumps low. Good luck.

Play ▸

Strange Company

The Nyetalax are spreading. Your crew is weird, and some of them will betray you. Get the barge to the coast.

WASD to move, mouse to aim and attack, Space to dodge. E searches, T talks, R is your special. No cure for infection, so check arms before you recruit.
